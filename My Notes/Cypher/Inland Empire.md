##### Intro Ideas:

Steal the thing with the Anything Else podcast that used to clip rants that people went into where they said "Anything Else" to then go into the music for the podcast.

Except instead it is explained that the intro always has subtitles, and then it will always cut into the title of Inland Empire by taking some combination of someone's sentence having an I and an E. For example:

A professor talking about some kind of lore to the city, which comes to an end when he verbally says IE.

Two businessmen talking about how to pitch this bad quarter, choosing to title it Inverse Expansion instead of downsizing.

Two investigators saying that they can't accuse a dirty cop unless they Irrefutable Evidence.
