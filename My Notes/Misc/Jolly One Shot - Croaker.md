Starting at level 5

I am a [High Elf](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/species:elf) [Artificer](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/artificer:main) with the subclass of [Artillerist](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/artificer:artillerist) and the [Familiar Trainer](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/background:familiar-trainer) background. I am Chaotic Good.

A high elf who grew up in a peaceful hippy-style commune that abhorred all violence. They were threatened by (Giants?) and Croaker elected to put together what pieces they knew to defend them and lead them all to safety.

After they were safe, the group declared (and he agreed) that his familiarity with violence was something that couldn't be undone, so he gave them all a farewell and left, eventually becoming the gun-toting Artificer he is today.

My AC with Scale Mail is 17 because of 2 from DEX and 1 from the cloak.

My Spell Save is 8 + 3 + 3+ so 14
My Spell Attack is +6
