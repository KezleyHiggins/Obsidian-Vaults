A running ledger of every Debt between the PCs and the NPCs of Miami, compiled from Sessions 3–15 and the [[First Faction Turn]].

> **Reading this:** "Owed **to**" means the NPC can call it in on the PC. "Owed **by**" means the PC holds the marker. Non-Debt obligations (soul bargains, demonic contracts, the death-apparition's bargain) are tracked separately at the bottom — they don't behave like Debts, but they'll kill someone just the same.

## [[Special Agent Morris]]

**Owes:**
- **[[Delaware]]** — extorted a Debt out of him in Session 6. Delaware also eased his pain when he was wounded in Session 5, and got punched for it.
- **[[Theresa Falco]]** — Session 11. While being healed of his critical damage, Morris gave up more information than he should have. Awkward, given he closed her missing-person case by sending [[Max]]'s photo upstairs.
- **[[Harry Johnson]]** — for information about [[Open Door Associates]]. Harry was killed by [[Fizbal]] and [[Anouk]] on 4 June 2018, so there is nobody left to call this one in — unless [[Olivia Wilderson]] or Open Door decide the debt was theirs to inherit.

**Is owed by:**
- **[[The Circumstance]] (Digby)** — Morris had to give up a CI to get [[Gravel]]'s location in Session 9, but in the end didn't give the name away. Digby owes him for it and *expects work in return*; he believes Morris knows who killed the Spring Prince. Morris has since put him on the Fae Prince's murder, with strict instructions that nothing trace back to Morris or the DOJ.
- **[[Nathan Atlantic]]** — for getting him out of trouble with [[The Draconic Order Of Justice]]. Currently unpaid and increasingly relevant: Morris is pulling Nathan's rap sheet (Session 15), and [[Kimberly]] intends to feed Nathan to the death-apparition.

## [[Kimberly]]

**Owes:**
- **[[Delaware]]** — Session 6. Her trauma attack did faint damage to him, and the Debt landed on her.
- **Donnie** — Session 14. She attacked [[Toni]]'s Spring Fae bodyguard. Donnie is already done with Toni over Gloves being abandoned, so he has little reason to be gentle about collecting.

**Is owed by:**
- **[[Noel]]** — revealed in Session 5, when he handed [[Anouk]] the 'PROBLEM' envelope. Unspecified and, so far, uncalled.

**Settled:**
- **[[Joseph Savino]]** — she owed him for interfering with an important business meeting. He *called it in*, and she told him everything about what she was going through with her brother. Savino passed it up the chain to his monarch, who passed it to [[Edith Falco]] — which is why Edith escalated and started making [[Special Agent Morris]]'s life difficult. **The debt is paid; the consequences are not.**

## [[Toni]]

**Owes:**
- **[[Andrea Rojas]]** — one Debt for information on [[Open Door Associates]].
- **[[Harry Johnson]]** — one Debt for undercutting his mark and ruining his scam. Harry is dead; the marker is orphaned.

**Is owed by:**
- **[[Becket]]** (the Fae formerly known as Roman) — taken as a Boon/Payout on the "When In Rome…" scheme: *Throw your weight around*. Worth noting that Becket has since sold Toni out, riding with [[Nathan Atlantic]] to come for Toni's left hand in Session 13. **This Debt is unspent leverage on a man who has already betrayed him.**
- **[[The Circumstance]] (Digby)** — came to owe Toni a Debt, surfaced during [[Anouk]]'s Session 7 Consult Your Contacts. Digby therefore owes both Toni *and* Morris.

**Cancelled:**
- **[[Olivia Wilderson]]** — Toni owed the head of Open Door Associates a Debt (struck moments before [[Harry Johnson]]'s death, Session 3). He cleared it as the second payout on "When In Rome…": *Pay down your debts*.

## [[Fizbal]]

**Owes:**
- **[[Santiago]]** — Session 14, for withholding information. His own apprentice.
- **[[Delaware]]** — Session 14, for withholding information. Delaware now holds Debts on Fizbal, Morris *and* Kimberly — three of the five original PCs.

**Outstanding and unnamed:**
- Fizbal's **Tend To Your Business** faction move in the [[First Faction Turn]] came up a Miss: *"an NPC interrupts your day-to-day business to cash in a Debt you hoped they had forgotten about, mixing you up in a mess within your Circle you'd rather have avoided."* **The NPC has never been named.** This is a loaded gun sitting in the Power circle.

## [[Anouk]]

No Debts recorded in either direction.

- Session 4's note that *"a vampire is going to be owed a debt"* following the party dispatching [[Harry Johnson]] has never been resolved to a named creditor. The likeliest claimant is [[Olivia Wilderson]] or Open Door Associates, who lost a realtor to Anouk and Fizbal on 4 June 2018.

## [[Flynt Locke]]

**Owes:**
- **[[Max]]** — Session 15. Max provided the name of **Bill "the Swamp Wizard" Keith**, and is owed a Debt for it. Flynt's first entry in the ledger, and it puts him immediately in the pocket of Toni's business rival and Morris's old partner.

**Informal (not Debts, but he'd argue otherwise):**
- **Little George** — Flynt reckons he owes him for finding the money that paid for Mama Gilly's heart surgery.
