
Custom Moves

# Morris

When you spend your time amongst the elites of Mortalis and Power to decide who will be the next leader of the City Guard, roll +HEART. In addition; for all the questions below, if the answer is yes take a +1 modifier, if the answer is 'no' then take a -1 modifier.

- Are you willing to wholeheartedly and confidently say you think he's the best man for the job?
- Are you willing to wholeheartedly and confidently say that you still trust him like when you were partners?
- Are you willing to admit that the City Guard are just as important to the city than the DOJ?

On a hit, Max wins out the vote. The rubber stamping will happen on Monday, but he's got the job. On a 7-9, news of your meddling in Mortalis affairs is going to offend someone in the DOJ. On a miss, prepare for the worst.

# Kimberly

When you participate in an attack on the Wizards to reclaim the lost sanctuary, roll +BLOOD. On a hit, the sanctuary belongs to the Victims of Miami once more. On a 10+, losses are minimal and you distinguish yourself as capable under pressure.

On a 7-9, losses are harsh but you manage to through it without anything catastrophically bad happening. There will not be much enthusiasm for fighting in the near future.

On a miss, the sanctuary remains lost and you need to tell me who is permanently removed from the city in the skirmish while trying to get their people out; Ulysses or Noel.
