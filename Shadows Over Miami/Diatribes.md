[[Hazel]]

I think its something my son would have wanted me to do. I lost him due to some political manoeuvring by my Monarch years ago. I wasn't even there to stop it. Time moved on. The worlds kept revolving. And I'm stuck in the same room, staring at an empty crib.

And I see him there.

And he's always smiling. The memory is like ash in my eyes.

I look out at the wilds of Arcadia or the Stars of Earth and I know that everything everywhere is looking at the same light whether they realize it or not.

And if I were gone and he were here, he'd be thinking the same thing. 

So I want to know, when you look at the Stars or leaves in the wind do you feel everyone looking back, or have you just not noticed yet?
