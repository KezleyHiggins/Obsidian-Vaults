# Special Agent Morris of the DOJ

Played by Darren.


He swore his oath because he believes that that’s the best way to improve society because he comes from a cop family (mundane) and FBI and stuff. He was head hunted out of the actual police for this position.

Been in the city his whole life (nearly forty)

The person who trained him in the ways of the order was a man who is now retired, who's blind and has supernatural senses, walking stick etc.

He’s different because he’s not immortal, and he was given power not born with it

He is presently investigating a missing daughter case of a very powerful rich Oracle

---

### Where He Stands (as of Session 15)

The Theresa Falco case was closed upstairs after he sent in [[Max]]'s photo of her crying next to [[Gravel]] (Session 8). He has since turned to the Spring Prince's murder and the bank-heist shooting.

He was healed of his critical damage in Session 11 by a DOJ healer, whose number Max passed him. In the process he gave [[Theresa Falco]] more information than he should have and now **owes her a Debt**.

He portrayed the Golem incident to the office as him simply being in a room with "a patient", staying vague about who (Session 14).

[[The Circumstance]] is looking into the Fae Prince's murder for him, under explicit instruction that nobody can know he is directed by Morris or handled by the DOJ — the goal is a "credible lead".

**Session 15:** The gun has finally been examined — it is enormously expensive and somewhat sentient, and it dislikes both Morris and [[Anouk]]. He wants to put in a request to get some items out of the storage locker, and is heading to the DOJ HQ, where he intends to pull [[Nathan Atlantic]]'s rap sheet.
